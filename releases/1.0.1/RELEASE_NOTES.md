# ToneRelay for Android 1.0.1

- Added Simplified Chinese, English, German, Japanese, French, and Spanish. Choose an app language from About or follow your phone’s preferences.
- Improved multi-speaker streaming and the layout of device cards, speaker settings, and stereo controls, especially on smaller screens.
- Updated playback buffer presets to Ultra 45 ms, Low latency 100 ms, and Stable 150 ms. Stable is suited to music and extended playback. HomePod can use a larger compatibility buffer, which the app displays when applicable. Actual time to sound depends on the source, network, and speaker.

Android 10 or later. Install over the previous official app to retain settings and activation.

## 安卓版 1.0.1

- 新增简体中文、英语、德语、日语、法语和西班牙语，支持跟随手机语言或在「关于」中手动切换。
- 改善多设备投送，以及设备列表、音箱设置和左右声道在小屏和长译文下的排版。
- 播放缓冲档位为极速 45 ms、低延迟 100 ms 和稳定 150 ms。稳定档适合歌曲播放、长时间播放。HomePod 使用较大的兼容缓冲时，界面会显示当前目标。实际出声时间取决于音源、网络和音箱。

支持 Android 10 及以上。可直接覆盖官方旧版，保留设置和授权。
